CONTACT & SUPPORT

Contact Alter Hub

Choose the right support channel and include enough information to make technical, billing, or documentation questions easier to resolve.

Official contact channels

Email support

Use email for account-specific, billing, privacy, or detailed technical questions that should not be posted publicly.

Discord community

Use the official server for public announcements, community questions, and support channels. Do not post keys or payment identifiers publicly.

Open the official Discord server

YouTube

Public videos, showcases, and update content are available through the Alter Hub channel.

Visit the YouTube channel

Documentation correction

For an outdated guide, send the page URL, the heading that is wrong, what you saw instead, and a screenshot when useful.

Report a documentation issue

For technical support

A useful technical report includes the supported game, the access tier, the page or screen where the issue appeared, the exact visible error, and the last action that worked. Say whether the problem started after a game update or a recent Alter Hub change.

Do not send your generated key unless support specifically provides a secure process that requires it. A screenshot should hide keys, email addresses, transaction IDs, or other private information.

For billing and subscription questions

Use email rather than a public Discord channel. Include the plan name and a clear description of the problem, but avoid sending passwords or unnecessary financial information. The Terms of Service explain the site's current purchase and subscription rules.

Privacy and legal requests

The Privacy Policy explains the categories of information the site may process and how to contact Alter Hub about privacy questions. Legal and privacy requests should be sent by email so they are not mixed with public support discussion.
