1. Scope of This Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to information processed through the Alter Hub website, checkout process, support systems, license services and related online services.

By using Alter Hub, you acknowledge the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We May Collect

Information you provide

  • Email address.
  • Username or display name.
  • Discord username or identifier, where provided.
  • Purchase and order information.
  • Support messages and communications.
  • Information submitted during payment verification.

Information collected automatically

  • IP address.
  • Browser type and browser version.
  • Operating system and device information.
  • Approximate location based on IP address.
  • Pages viewed and actions performed.
  • Referring pages and URLs.
  • Session identifiers and timestamps.
  • Error logs, security events and diagnostic information.

License and product information

  • License key status.
  • Activation and validation timestamps.
  • Associated account or device identifiers.
  • Plan type and expiration date.
  • Suspected abuse or unauthorized sharing signals.

3. Payment Information

Payments may be processed by third-party providers such as PayPal or another payment processor shown at checkout.

Alter Hub generally does not receive or store full credit card numbers, bank account credentials or payment account passwords.

We may receive limited transaction information, including:

  • Transaction identifier.
  • Payment status.
  • Amount and currency.
  • Billing name or email where supplied by the processor.
  • Fraud and risk information.
  • Refund, dispute and chargeback status.

Payment providers process information according to their own privacy policies.

4. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Process and verify purchases.
  • Deliver and manage license keys.
  • Activate subscriptions and product access.
  • Respond to support requests.
  • Detect fraud, abuse and unauthorized key sharing.
  • Investigate chargebacks and payment disputes.
  • Secure Alter Hub systems and user accounts.
  • Diagnose errors and improve performance.
  • Understand website usage and user experience.
  • Comply with legal, tax and accounting obligations.
  • Enforce the Terms of Service.

5. Legal Bases for Processing

Depending on your location, Alter Hub may process information based on:

  • Contract: processing needed to deliver a purchase or service.
  • Legitimate interests: fraud prevention, security, service improvement and enforcement of rights.
  • Legal obligation: compliance with tax, accounting, regulatory or lawful disclosure requirements.
  • Consent: where consent is legally required for cookies, marketing or another specific activity.

6. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Alter Hub may use cookies, local storage, session storage, pixels or similar technologies for:

  • Maintaining sessions.
  • Remembering settings and preferences.
  • Protecting against abuse.
  • Measuring site traffic and performance.
  • Supporting checkout functionality.

You may be able to disable cookies through your browser. However, some website, login or checkout features may stop functioning correctly.

7. When Information May Be Shared

Alter Hub does not sell personal information.

Information may be shared with:

  • Payment processors.
  • Hosting and infrastructure providers.
  • Cloud security and content delivery providers.
  • Email and support service providers.
  • Analytics and error-monitoring providers.
  • Professional advisers where necessary.
  • Authorities where disclosure is legally required.

Service providers should only receive information reasonably necessary to perform their assigned function.

Information may also be disclosed during a business reorganization, acquisition, sale, merger or transfer of assets, subject to applicable law.

8. Fraud Prevention and Payment Disputes

Alter Hub may collect and preserve order, access, activation, IP, device and communication records to investigate suspected fraud, unauthorized purchases, key sharing, refunds or chargebacks.

Relevant records may be provided to payment processors, banks, dispute-resolution providers or lawful authorities when reasonably necessary to defend a transaction or prevent abuse.

9. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy.

Retention periods may vary based on:

  • Whether a license remains active.
  • Security and fraud-prevention needs.
  • Chargeback and dispute deadlines.
  • Tax and accounting requirements.
  • Legal claims and enforcement needs.

Certain records may be retained after an account or key is terminated where needed to prevent repeated abuse or satisfy a legal obligation.

10. International Data Transfers

Alter Hub and its providers may process information in countries different from your country of residence.

Where required, reasonable safeguards may be used to protect information transferred internationally.

11. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal information.
  • Correct inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of certain information.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.
  • Request a portable copy of certain information.
  • Complain to an applicable privacy authority.

These rights may be limited where retention is required for fraud prevention, legal compliance, dispute handling or the establishment and defense of legal claims.

Alter Hub may request reasonable verification before acting on a privacy request.

12. Children's Privacy

Alter Hub is not intended for children who are below the minimum age required to consent to online data processing or make purchases under applicable law.

Users below the required age should only access Alter Hub with the involvement and permission of a parent or legal guardian.

13. Data Security

Alter Hub uses reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration or disclosure.

These measures may include access controls, encryption, monitoring, rate limiting, security logging and trusted infrastructure providers.

No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Users are responsible for protecting their email accounts, passwords, license keys and devices.

14. Third-Party Links and Services

Alter Hub may contain links to external websites or integrate external platforms such as PayPal, Discord, Cloudflare or analytics providers.

Their privacy practices are controlled by their own policies. Alter Hub is not responsible for how independent third parties collect or use information.

15. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes in Alter Hub, legal requirements, technology or data-processing practices.

The updated policy will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
