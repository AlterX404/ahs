Dated release history

Blox Fruits added

September 29, 2024

V1.0.0

  • Main: fighting style acquisition, Boss Farm, and additional automation
  • Players: spectate, teleport, player farm, Aim Bot, bounty farm
  • Auto Stats: Auto Stats and Fake Stats
  • Teleport: worlds, Sea Beast, islands
  • Dungeon: dungeon farm, awakening, chip purchase, raid start, teleport
  • Devil Fruit: fruit shop, Fruit Sniper, fruit finding and storage actions, random fruit
  • Shop access to many purchasable items
  • Misc: server hop/rejoin, stat boosters, team changer, FPS tools, and ESP categories

Rework announced

May 1, 2025
  • A full Blox Fruits rework was announced as upcoming

Blox Fruits script reworked

May 21, 2025
  • Main: Auto Farm, Mastery Farm, Mob Farm, Boss Farm
  • Farming: Elite Hunter, Bone Farming, Pirate Raid, Observation, Chest Farm, Cake Farm, Materials
  • Items: Fighting Style, Gun and Sword, Other
  • Settings, Local Player, Hold Skills, Sea Events, Stack Sea, Crafts, Dragon Dojo
  • Stats, Race V4, Combat, Raid, ESP, Teleport, Shop, Fruit, Misc, and Server tabs

Loading fix

June 11, 2025
  • Blox Fruits script not loading issue fixed
  • Reported as working normally after the fix

Maintenance fix

August 31, 2025
  • Blox Fruits fixed alongside Phantom Forces

Before reporting a problem

Check whether the issue started after a game update and whether Alter Hub itself still opens. Include the game name, the feature or tab involved, the visible error text, and the date you tested. Do not publish private keys or account credentials.

For access problems, use the Key System guide. For general errors, use Troubleshooting or the Support page.
